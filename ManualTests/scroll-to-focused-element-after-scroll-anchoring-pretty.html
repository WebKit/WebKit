Spotify queue-panel "hop" repro

Press Open Queue. The flow:

  1. Mount the "Next from" section into the queue list. Visible content: "Next from: Rock Artist" at the top of the panel, scroll position = 0.
  2. Wait one paint.
  3. Prepend the "Queue" header, "Now playing" label, and the "Title 1" row above the existing content. The browser's scroll-anchoring algorithm shifts scrollTop automatically to keep the previously-visible content stable, so "Next from" stays at the top. Then focus() on the newly-inserted "Title 1" row.

Unpatched Safari: the focus() schedules a 0 s Timer for the focused-element scroll. The next paint happens before the Timer fires, so the list paints with "Next from" still at the top (anchored scrollTop). One Timer-fire later, scroll jumps to scrollTop = 0 — "Now playing: Title 1" snaps into view at the top. The hop.

Chrome: focus-scroll commits before the paint that would have shown the anchored position, so the first paint shows Now Playing at the top.

Patched Safari: our rendering-update step runs the deferred focus-scroll inside the same rendering update as the content insertion, so paint commits at scrollTop = 0 from the start.
