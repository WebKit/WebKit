rxeslotted
Verifies that editability propagates correctly when shadow trees are nested.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


Nothing is editable to begin with.
PASS checkEditability(reslotted) is "isContentEditable: false, canTypeInto: false"
PASS checkEditability(slottingBox) is "isContentEditable: false, canTypeInto: false"

An editable host reaches its content through both slots, but not into its shadow trees.
PASS checkEditability(reslotted) is "isContentEditable: true, canTypeInto: true"
PASS checkEditability(slottingBox) is "isContentEditable: false, canTypeInto: false"

Even if both shadow trees are made fully editable, the reslotted content is still not editable.
PASS checkEditability(reslotted) is "isContentEditable: false, canTypeInto: false"
PASS checkEditability(slottingBox) is "isContentEditable: true, canTypeInto: true"
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
