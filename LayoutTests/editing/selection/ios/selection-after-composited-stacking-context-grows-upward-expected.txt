Verifies that the caret stays in place when content is inserted above an editable field, inside a bottom-anchored stacking context that has a composited negative z-index child. To test manually, focus the field and press Escape; the caret should not move.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".

PASS caretRectBeforeInsertion.top is caretRectAfterInsertion.top
PASS caretRectBeforeInsertion.left is caretRectAfterInsertion.left
PASS caretRectBeforeInsertion.width is caretRectAfterInsertion.width
PASS caretRectBeforeInsertion.height is caretRectAfterInsertion.height
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
