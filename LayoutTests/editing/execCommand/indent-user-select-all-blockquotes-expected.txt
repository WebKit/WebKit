This tests indenting into a new blockquote when blockquotes are styled as -webkit-user-select: all. Editability now overrides user-select: all, so the new blockquote is editable and the indent goes through. See indent-user-select-all-blockquotes-legacy.html for the legacy behavior, where user-select: all made the blockquote uneditable and we bailed out instead of indenting.


On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".
