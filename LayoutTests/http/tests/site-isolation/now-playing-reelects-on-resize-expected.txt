Under site isolation, the GPU process re-runs the NowPlaying election when a candidate's eligibility changes even if its NowPlayingInfo does not. A cross-site subframe video plays first and the same-sized main-frame video plays second, so the main frame is elected (both are main-content-sized, and it was interacted with more recently). After the main-frame video is paused and shrunk below the main-content threshold, the subframe video must be elected because it is now the only main-content-sized candidate. A paused video's info is frozen, so only candidacy reported independently of the info-dedup path can trigger the re-election.
