A popup that its opener navigates to about:blank inherits the opener's origin, because the opener initiated the navigation. The opener can only modify the popup's DOM while the two are same-origin, so this checks that the about:blank still has the opener's origin after the popup goes back to it from a cross-site document.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".
