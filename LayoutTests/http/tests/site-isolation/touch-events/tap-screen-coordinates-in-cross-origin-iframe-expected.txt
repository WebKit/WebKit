A tap inside a cross-origin iframe must report screenX/screenY in top-level page coordinates rather than relative to the iframe.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


PASS eventScreenX - eventClientX is iframeOffsetX
PASS eventScreenY - eventClientY is iframeOffsetY
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
