After a potential tap in a cross-origin iframe is cancelled, long-pressing a word in that iframe should select it. The iframe's process must not keep the cancelled tap's target as its pending potential tap target.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


PASS The first tap was not committed as a single click.
PASS A word was selected inside the cross-origin iframe.
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
