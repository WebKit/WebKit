Tapping a button in the middle of a tall cross-origin iframe that has been scrolled should show a tap highlight over the button.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


PASS subframeScrollY is expectedSubframeScrollY
PASS buttonWasClicked is true
PASS highlightWidth is buttonWidth + 2 * tapHighlightInflationPerSide
PASS highlightHeight is buttonHeight + 2 * tapHighlightInflationPerSide
PASS highlightTop is expectedTop - tapHighlightInflationPerSide
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
