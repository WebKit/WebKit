Long-pressing text in a cross-site frame whose parent is itself a cross-site frame that is SCROLLED should select the word under the finger. The middle frame is the local root of its process, and unlike a main frame its view does not delegate scrolling to a native scroll view, so its scroll offset can be applied to the point twice.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".
