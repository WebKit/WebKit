A two-finger tap inside a cross-origin iframe should send the iframe a click with metaKey set, at the tapped point in the iframe's coordinates.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


PASS receivedClick.metaKey is true
PASS receivedClick.clientX is 200
PASS receivedClick.clientY is 150
PASS successfullyParsed is true
