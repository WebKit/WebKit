After a tap is committed in a cross-origin iframe, long-pressing a word in the main frame should select it. The main frame's process must not keep the iframe as its pending potential tap target.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


PASS The tap was dispatched in the cross-origin iframe.
PASS A word was selected in the main frame.
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
Hello world main frame text
