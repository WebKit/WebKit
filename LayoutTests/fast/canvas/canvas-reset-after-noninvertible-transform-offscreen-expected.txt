Resetting a 2D OffscreenCanvas context (reset(), or setting the canvas width) must restore an invertible identity transform, even if the transform was non-invertible before the reset.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


reset():
PASS pixel is [255,0,0,255]
PASS pixel is [255,0,0,255]

Setting the canvas width to its current value:
PASS pixel is [255,0,0,255]
PASS pixel is [255,0,0,255]
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
