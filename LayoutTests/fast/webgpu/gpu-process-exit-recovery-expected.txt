After the GPU process exits, navigator.gpu has to provide adapters again, in the page and in a worker, a new canvas has to get a WebGPU context before any adapter is requested, and canvases have to work with a device from the new adapter, including one that was configured with a lost device.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".
