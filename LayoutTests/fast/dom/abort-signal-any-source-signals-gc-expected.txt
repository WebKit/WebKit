Dependent signals created by AbortSignal.any() must survive the GC's parallel weak-sets pass once all of their source signals have been collected. JSAbortSignalOwner::isReachableFromOpaqueRoots() inspects AbortSignal::m_sourceSignals on every GC marker thread, so inspecting it must not free it.
