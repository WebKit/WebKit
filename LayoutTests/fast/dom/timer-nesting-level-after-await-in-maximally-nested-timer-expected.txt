Tests the nesting level of a setTimeout(callback, 0) when scheduled by another timer after an await. Code after an await runs in a microtask, not a timer task, so the HTML spec says the new timer shouldn't be considered nested.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


1) Awaiting an already settled promise
PASS levelInNestedTimer is maxNestingLevel
FAIL levelAfterAwait should be 0. Was 10.
FAIL newTimerIsThrottled should be false. Was true.
FAIL levelInNewTimer should be 1. Was 10.

2) Awaiting a promise that a timer settles immediately
PASS levelInNestedTimer is maxNestingLevel
FAIL levelAfterAwait should be 0. Was 10.
FAIL newTimerIsThrottled should be false. Was true.
FAIL levelInNewTimer should be 1. Was 10.

3) Awaiting a promise that a microtask settles

FAIL levelAfterAwait should be 0. Was 10.
FAIL newTimerIsThrottled should be false. Was true.
FAIL levelInNewTimer should be 1. Was 10.
