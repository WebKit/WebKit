Verifies that touches that begin near a selection handle are still terminated with touchend/pointerup or touchcancel/pointercancel, even when the page cannot prevent the native selection gesture.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".

PASS initialSelectionLength is > 0

Dragging the selection end handle.
PASS Touch and pointer sequence ended after dragging the selection handle.
PASS expandedSelectionLength is > initialSelectionLength

Tapping near the selection end handle.
PASS Touch and pointer sequence ended after tapping near the selection handle.
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
