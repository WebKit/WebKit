Verifies that web content receives stylus touches while a finger is touching the screen, and vice versa. To test manually, rest a finger on the gray area and then touch it with Apple Pencil (and vice versa); both the finger and the pencil should be reported, and each should be the primary pointer of its type.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".



Finger, then pencil:
pointerdown: pointerType=touch isPrimary=true
touchstart: touches=[direct] changedTouches=[direct]
pointerdown: pointerType=pen isPrimary=true
touchstart: touches=[direct, stylus] changedTouches=[stylus]
pointermove: pointerType=pen isPrimary=true
pointerup: pointerType=pen isPrimary=true
touchend: touches=[direct] changedTouches=[stylus]
pointerup: pointerType=touch isPrimary=true
touchend: touches=[] changedTouches=[direct]

Pencil, then finger:
pointerdown: pointerType=pen isPrimary=true
touchstart: touches=[stylus] changedTouches=[stylus]
pointermove: pointerType=pen isPrimary=true
pointerdown: pointerType=touch isPrimary=true
touchstart: touches=[direct, stylus] changedTouches=[direct]
pointermove: pointerType=pen isPrimary=true
pointerup: pointerType=pen isPrimary=true
touchend: touches=[direct] changedTouches=[stylus]
pointerup: pointerType=touch isPrimary=true
touchend: touches=[] changedTouches=[direct]
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
