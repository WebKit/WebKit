The repaint rect of a grid container does not include the box-shadow of a grid item that paints through its own layer.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


(repaint rects
  (rect 100 100 60 30)
)

PASS successfullyParsed is true
