The repaint rect of a grid container includes the box-shadow of a nested grid container that is its grid item.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


(repaint rects
  (rect 70 70 120 90)
)

PASS repaintRects.indexOf('(rect 70 70 120 90)') != -1 is true
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
