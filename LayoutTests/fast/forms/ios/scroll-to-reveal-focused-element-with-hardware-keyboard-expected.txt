
Verifies that focusing a text field below the visible area scrolls it into view when a hardware keyboard is attached. This test requires WebKitTestRunner.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".

PASS document.activeElement is textField
PASS pageYOffset + innerHeight is > textField.offsetTop
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
