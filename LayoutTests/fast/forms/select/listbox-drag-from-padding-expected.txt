A drag that starts in the padding of a listbox, outside of any option, should not change the selection or fire change events.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


Dragging sideways out of a multiple-selection listbox:
PASS selectedOptionsText("multiple") is ""
PASS changeEventCount is 0

Autoscrolling a single-selection listbox:
PASS selectedOptionsText("single") is "Option 2"
PASS changeEventCount is 0

Dragging onto an option of a single-selection listbox that cannot scroll:
PASS selectedOptionsText("unscrollable") is "Option 1"
PASS changeEventCount is 0
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
