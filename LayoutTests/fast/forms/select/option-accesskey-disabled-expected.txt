Tests that an option cannot be chosen by its access key when the option, its optgroup, or its select is disabled. To test this manually, press <alt>+b, <alt>+d, <alt>+f and <alt>+h in this order (on macOS, press <ctrl>+<opt> instead of <alt>).

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


PASS enabled.value is "b"
PASS disabledSelect.value is "c"
PASS disabledOption.value is "e"
PASS disabledOptGroup.value is "g"
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
