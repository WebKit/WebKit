Removing a shadow host must not leave the surviving shadow tree pointing at the freed host. This tests both the observability verdict for a removed shadow root and the shadow-including-root scrub in removeShadowRootSlow. The test passes if it does not crash.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".

PASS child.getRootNode({composed: true}) is shadowRootWithChild.host
