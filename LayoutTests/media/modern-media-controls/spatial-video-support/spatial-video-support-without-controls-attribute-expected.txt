Testing that a projected video is projected without the controls attribute, and that it gets none of the control chrome. A video that opts out with projection=none, or that has no projection at all, gets no media controls shadow tree.

On success, you will see a series of "PASS" messages, followed by "TEST COMPLETE".


PASS hasMediaControls(projected) is true

A video that opts out of projection does not need them:
PASS hasMediaControls(optedOut) is false

Neither does a video with no projection at all:
PASS hasMediaControls(plain) is false

The projection is rendered:
PASS projectionCanvas(projected) became different from null

...and none of the control chrome is shown:
PASS controlChromeChildCount(projected) became 0
PASS successfullyParsed is true

TEST COMPLETE
