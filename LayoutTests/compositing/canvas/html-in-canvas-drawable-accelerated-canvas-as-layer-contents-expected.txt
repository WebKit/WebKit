Tests the layers of 2D canvases with content="drawable" for {small, large} x {no options, willReadFrequently}, before and after they become not drawable. An accelerated drawable canvas shows its pixels through a contents layer, while its own layer still paints so its drawable content gets recorded. An unaccelerated one has no contents layer. A canvas that is not drawable paints into the page layer.
